About Uddah

Uddah is a student project that offers a simple idea: rent the tool you need by the day instead of buying it. Our goal is to help people finish their home jobs easily while saving money and space.

Jump to: Vision & Mission | Our Values

Our Vision

To make useful tools available to everyone, whenever they need them.

Our Mission

To provide quality, checked tools at a fair daily price, with clear rental terms and friendly service.

Our Values

  • Saving: pay only for what you use.
  • Quality: every tool is checked before each rental.
  • Trust: clear terms explained in our rental policy.
  • Sustainability: sharing tools means less waste.
