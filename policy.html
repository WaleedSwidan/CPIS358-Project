Rental Policy

Please read our rental terms before renting any equipment from Uddah.

Jump to: Rental Period | Deposit | Late Return | Damage | FAQ

1. Rental Period

  1. Equipment is rented by the day (24 hours).
  2. The rental day starts when you receive the equipment.
  3. You can extend the rental by contacting us before the end of the day.

2. Deposit

A refundable deposit is required for every rental. The deposit is returned when:

  • The equipment is returned on time.
  • The equipment is returned clean and in good condition.
  • All parts and accessories are included:
    • Batteries and chargers
    • Drill bits and attachments
    • Carrying cases

3. Late Return

Late return fees
Delay Fee
Up to 3 hours No fee
3 to 24 hours One extra rental day
More than 24 hours One extra day for each day of delay

4. Damage and Loss

The renter is responsible for the equipment during the rental period. Repair or replacement costs may be deducted from the deposit.

Note: Please report any problem with the equipment as soon as you receive it.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my rental?

Yes, you can cancel for free before receiving the equipment.

Do I need an account to rent?

Yes, please create an account first.

How do I contact Uddah?

Visit our Contact Us page.
