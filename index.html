Welcome to Uddah

Uddah is a daily tool and equipment rental website. Instead of buying an expensive tool that you will only use once or twice, you can rent it from Uddah for the days you need and return it when you finish.

The Problem

Many people need a tool for a small job, such as installing a shelf or fixing something at home. Buying a new tool costs a lot of money, takes space to store, and is rarely used again.

Our Solution

Uddah lets you rent the tool you need by the day, at a low price. You save money and storage space, and you always get a tool that is checked and ready to use.

How It Works

  1. Create an account on Uddah.
  2. Choose the tool you need from our products.
  3. Pick up the tool and use it for the days you need.
  4. Return the tool and get your deposit back.

Why Uddah?

  • Save money: pay only for the days you use.
  • No storage: no need to keep big tools at home.
  • Quality tools: every tool is checked before rental.
  • Clear terms: read our rental policy.

Did you know? Most home tools are used only a few times a year.
